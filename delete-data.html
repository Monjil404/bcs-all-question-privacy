The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone. This page explains how to delete that data, and how to ask Tech Easy Life to delete the little information that reaches our server.

1. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → BCS All Question.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (or Clear data). This deletes your progress, bookmarks, test results and settings.
  3. Or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes everything the app stored and ends its notification subscription.

2. Ask us to delete data held on our server

  1. Email phpmy404@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – BCS All Question”.
  2. Because the app has no accounts, tell us what you want deleted, for example a question error report (the approximate date and the question), or your notification record.
  3. We reply within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We do not ask for passwords or ID documents.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: question error reports you sent (reason, comment, app version) and your push-notification record at OneSignal.
  • Not kept by us: we have no name, email, phone number or account for you.
  • Server request logs: standard technical logs (IP address, time, address requested) are deleted automatically within 30 days and are not linked to you.
  • Advertising data: held by Google AdMob under Google's policy. Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and see how Google uses ad data.
  • Question corrections: if your report led to a corrected question, the correction stays in the question bank, but the report itself is deleted.
