1. Who we are

BCS All Question (বিসিএস প্রশ্নব্যাংক) is a free study app for Bangladeshi government job exams — BCS, primary teacher, NTRCA, bank, non-cadre and grade 11–20 recruitment. It is developed by Monirujjaman Monjil (Tech Easy Life), who is responsible for the processing described here. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information we do not collect

  • There are no user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email address, photos, contacts, messages or files.
  • The app does not request or use your location (GPS), camera or microphone.
  • We do not collect health, financial, religious or other sensitive personal information.

3. Information stored only on your device

Your study data is saved on your phone and is never sent to us: answers and scores, test results and history, reading progress, bookmarks, the list of questions you got wrong, your study streak, settings (theme, text size, answer display, reminder time), closed announcements and the date you first opened the app (used only to decide when to ask for a Play review). The daily study reminder is scheduled on the phone itself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of this.

4. Information processed to run the app

5. Advertising

  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may use the Advertising ID and cookies to show ads, which may be personalised if you allow it. See how Google uses information in ads.
  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later from the app's Settings (“বিজ্ঞাপনের গোপনীয়তা”).
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • We do not use ads to target children.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state — to download content and show ads.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later asks you) — for push notifications and the daily study reminder. The app works fully if you refuse.
  • Run at start-up (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to set the daily reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android's ad services permissions) — used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notification support added by the notification service: vibration, wake lock, app icon badges and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for purposes other than those above. Information reaches only the service providers listed in section 4, who process it under their own privacy policies: Google and OneSignal. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Security

The released app talks to our server only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Study data on your phone stays in the app's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information described here.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings to delete everything the app stored.
  • Notifications: turn notifications off for the app in Android Settings; uninstalling ends the notification subscription. You may also ask us to delete your notification record.
  • Question reports: kept only as long as needed to correct the content.
  • Advertising data: managed by Google; reset or delete your Advertising ID as described in section 5.
  • To request deletion of any information we hold, email phpmy404@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, include the approximate date and what you sent (for example, a question report) so we can find it.

10. Children

The app is made for adults preparing for job exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

Job circulars, current affairs and notices link to their official or original sources (for example government websites and application portals). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
